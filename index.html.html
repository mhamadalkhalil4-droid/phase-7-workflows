3REACTIVATION OR NEW MANDATE
Codes: E (Card Blocked) · N (Dormant Account) · X (Blocked by Judiciary) · Y (Blocked by Law Enforcement) · Z (Blocked by Central Bank)  ·  Both Bank Submissions and Payer Reminders counters
ℹ️ Message gives payer two clearly labelled options: confirm reactivation (→ auto-retry immediately) OR update payment details for a new mandate. After a merchant-configured threshold of bank submissions, the reactivation option is removed and only the new mandate link remains.
ℹ️ When payer clicks the reactivation link → Bank Submissions +1 · Payer Reminders reset to 0.

4DDA CANCELLED — Merchant Notification & Ops Action
Code: O (DDA Cancelled)  ·  No automated action · Merchant notified immediately · Ops decides next step
ℹ️ The bank has already cancelled the direct debit authority on their side. There is nothing to retry. The merchant is notified and the ops team handles next steps — either cancel the mandate on the system directly, or reissue a new mandate.
